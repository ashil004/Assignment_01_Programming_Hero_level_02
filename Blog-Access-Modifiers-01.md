Think of access modifiers as security guards for your data. A public label means anyone can see or change the data from anywhere. A private label means the data is a complete secret, and only its own class can use it. A protected label is like a family secret; outsiders cannot touch it, but your child classes can use it. Finally, readonly means you can look at the data once when it is made, but nobody can change it ever again. These simple rules keep your code safe and clean.
